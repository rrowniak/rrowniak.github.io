{%- comment -%} Prefix for links to site-root paths ("", "../", "…") so that pages work both when served from a domain root and when opened directly from disk (file://). Every page here lives at the root or one directory below it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign rel_url = page.url -%} {%- assign rel_last = rel_url | slice: -1, 1 -%} {%- assign rel_len = rel_url | split: "/" | size -%} {%- if rel_last == "/" -%} {%- assign rel_depth = rel_len | minus: 1 -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign rel_depth = rel_len | minus: 2 -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign rel = "" -%} {%- for i in (1..rel_depth) -%}{%- assign rel = rel | append: "../" -%}{%- endfor -%}
